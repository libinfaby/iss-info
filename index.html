06 — About

The biggest thing we've ever built in space

The International Space Station is a modular station in low Earth orbit — the largest crewed object ever assembled in space. It circles Earth roughly every 90 minutes at about 28,000 km/h.

Run by NASA, Roscosmos, ESA, JAXA and CSA, it has had people aboard continuously since November 2000, doing experiments in biology, physics, astronomy and meteorology that only work in microgravity. Read more at NASA →
